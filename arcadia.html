The short version

A few useful answers.

Is Arcadia a calendar or task manager?

It sits one layer above both. Arcadia uses your work and commitments to decide what deserves attention now.

What happens when I miss a task?

Arcadia checks urgency and future capacity, then moves the task to the safest available place.

Does it plan around sport and other commitments?

Yes. Classes, training, shifts and personal commitments shape the time Arcadia treats as available.

Why include sleep?

Because a plan that only works by borrowing from recovery is not a realistic plan.
